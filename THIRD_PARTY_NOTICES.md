# Build tools

Tools are downloaded directly from their authors' pinned releases, checked against
SHA-256 hashes in `tools-lock.json`, and kept in the ignored `tools/` directory.
They are not committed or included in the mod's ZIP.

- [retoc](https://github.com/trumank/retoc), v0.1.5, by trumank and contributors — MIT.
- [repak](https://github.com/trumank/repak), v0.2.3, by trumank and contributors — MIT OR Apache-2.0.
- [UnrealLocres](https://github.com/akintos/UnrealLocres), v1.1.1, by akintos and contributors. Refer to its upstream distribution for usage terms.

See the upstream projects for their full license texts and source code.
The game localization resources in `sources/` originate from Tokyo Xtreme Racer
and remain the property of their respective rights holders.
